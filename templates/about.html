ABOUT THE PROJECT

The AI Behind TruthLens

Powered by Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, and Explainable AI.

🎯 Mission

TruthLens AI is designed to combat misinformation using modern Artificial Intelligence. The system analyzes linguistic patterns, writing style, and statistical features before classifying articles as Real or Fake.

🚀 Vision

Build a trustworthy ecosystem where AI assists users in identifying misinformation quickly and accurately — making credible information accessible to everyone.
